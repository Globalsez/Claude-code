Why a law firm

Most rejections happen before the Court ever sees the document

An apostille authenticates a signature and a seal — it does not fix a document that was never in the right form. The commonest reasons a legalised document is rejected abroad are avoidable: the wrong signatory, an uncertified copy where an original was required, a power of attorney whose wording does not carry the authority the receiving country expects, or a corporate record that is out of date by the time it lands.

Because {{LEGAL_NAME}} is a firm of attorneys, we check the document against the purpose it is being sent for before it goes anywhere near the Registry. That is the part that saves the second trip.

  • Notarial services in house — no third-party handoff
  • Originals held in the firm's safe custody throughout
  • Covered by professional confidentiality
Straight answer

If you can do it yourself, we will say so

The Supreme Court issues apostilles at a fixed statutory fee, in person, on set days. If you are in Victoria holding a document that is already in acceptable form, walking it in yourself is cheaper than instructing us — and we will tell you that rather than take the fee.

Instruct us when the document needs retrieving, certifying, notarising or drafting first, when you are not in Seychelles, or when it has to be right the first time because a closing depends on it.
