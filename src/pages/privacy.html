Privacy

Privacy notice

What this website does with your information — and, more to the point, what it does not do.

This website collects nothing

There is no database behind this site, no analytics tracking, no advertising pixels and no cookies set by us. The pages are static files. Nothing you type here is stored on this website.

The enquiry form on the contact page is not submitted to a server. It assembles what you have typed into a message and hands it to WhatsApp — or, if you have no WhatsApp, to your own email application — for you to send. Until you press send in that application, the information has not gone anywhere.

What that means in practice

  • We do not know you visited unless you contact us.
  • We hold no record of enquiries you started but did not send.
  • There is no marketing list, because there is nothing collecting addresses for one.

Third parties you interact with directly

Three services are reached from this site, and each is governed by its own privacy terms, not ours:

  • WhatsApp — when you message us, WhatsApp (Meta) handles the message in transit and holds it on your device and ours.
  • PayPal — payment is taken by PayPal. We never see or hold your card details; PayPal tells us only that a payment was made, by whom and for what.
  • Google Fonts — the site's typefaces are served by Google, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers when the page loads.

Once you instruct us

When you send us documents or instructions, you stop being a website visitor and become a client or prospective client of {{LEGAL_NAME}}. From that point your information is held by the firm under the professional duty of confidentiality that applies to a legal practice, and under the firm's own client-care terms — which we will give you and which govern the relationship. This notice covers the website; it is not the firm's full privacy terms.

Original documents you send us are held in the firm's safe custody between receipt and dispatch, and are returned to you by tracked courier. We retain copies and correspondence for as long as we are required to, and no longer.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, and ask us to delete it where we are not required by law or professional rules to keep it. Ask through any of the channels on our contact page and we will respond.

Changes

If this notice changes, the change appears on this page. Because the site collects nothing, changes here are likely to be rare and minor.
