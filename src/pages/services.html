The work around the apostille

Often the apostille is the easy part. These are the things that actually hold a matter up, and we do all of them in house.

Document retrieval

Certified copies obtained from the Registrar of Companies, the Civil Status Office or the Supreme Court registry where you no longer hold the original.

Notarial services

Execution before a notary, certified true copies, affidavits and statutory declarations — prepared so that they will be accepted for apostille without a second attempt.

Drafting and review

Powers of attorney, board resolutions and declarations drafted or reviewed against the requirements of the country the document is going to.

Courier and logistics

Tracked international dispatch of originals, and collection from your agent or registered office in Seychelles where needed.
