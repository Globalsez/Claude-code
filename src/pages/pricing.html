What is and is not included

No fee you have not been told about

The price against each service covers our professional fee and the Supreme Court apostille fee for that document. Three things are quoted separately, because they depend on your matter and we will not pad a headline price with them:

  • Courier — at cost, by destination and weight. Scanned copies are always free.
  • Registry retrieval — where a record has to be obtained before it can be legalised.
  • Drafting — where a document has to be written or substantially redrawn, not merely legalised.

Multiple documents in one instruction are quoted as a set and cost less per document than the rates above. Ask, and we will price the whole pack.

Paying by bank transfer

If you would rather not use PayPal, ask for an invoice and we will send our account details with it. For corporate instructions and law-firm referrals we invoice on completion as standard.
