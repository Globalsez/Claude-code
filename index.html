Why a law firm

Most rejections happen before the Court ever sees the document

An apostille authenticates a signature and a seal — it does not fix a document that was never in the right form. The commonest reasons a legalised document is rejected abroad are avoidable: the wrong signatory, an uncertified copy where an original was required, a power of attorney whose wording does not carry the authority the receiving country expects, or a corporate record that is out of date by the time it lands.

Because Victoria Law Firm is a firm of attorneys, we check the document against the purpose it is being sent for before it goes anywhere near the Registry. That is the part that saves the second trip.

Questions

Answers before you ask

What is an apostille?

An apostille is a certificate attached to a public document that authenticates the signature and seal on it, so the document is recognised in every country party to the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention. Seychelles has been a party since 1979, and apostilles here are issued by the Supreme Court of Seychelles. Once a document carries an apostille, no further embassy or consular legalisation is needed in any of the 120+ member states.

Can I not just do this myself at the Supreme Court?

You can. The Supreme Court at Ile du Port issues apostilles for a fixed statutory fee, with walk-in submission on limited days and collection two business days later, in cash. What we do is everything around that: confirming the document is in a form the Court will accept, arranging notarisation or certified copies where the original cannot be surrendered, retrieving records from the Registry or Civil Status, attending Court on your behalf, and couriering the originals to you anywhere in the world. If you are in Seychelles with a document already in order, going yourself is often the cheapest route — and we will tell you so.

Do you need my original documents?

Usually yes for personal certificates, because the Court apostilles the original or a notarially certified copy of it. For company documents we can normally work from records we retrieve or from registered agent certified copies, so nothing of yours leaves your hands. Tell us what you have and we will confirm before you post anything.

How long does it take?

Most straightforward documents are completed in two to three business days from the point we have everything we need. Documents requiring retrieval from a registry, verification with an issuing institution, or notarial execution take longer. Express handling is available where the Court's sitting days allow it.

Do you handle documents from outside Seychelles?

We regularly handle British Virgin Islands corporate records and documents from other offshore jurisdictions. A document issued in another country must be apostilled by the competent authority of that country, not by Seychelles — but we coordinate that process for you and can act as the single point of contact for a multi-jurisdiction set.

How do I pay?

By PayPal on this site, using the card or PayPal balance of your choice, or by bank transfer if you prefer. Payment is to Victoria Law Firm. For instructions involving retrieval, drafting or multiple documents we issue a written quotation first, so you always know the total before anything is paid.

Is my document safe with you?

Originals are held in the firm's safe custody between lodgement and dispatch, and are sent by tracked international courier with the tracking number sent to you the day they leave us. We are a law firm: your documents and your instructions are covered by professional confidentiality.
